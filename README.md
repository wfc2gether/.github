## 🌟 What is wfc2gether?

> **Tired of studying or working from home in complete isolation?**  
> We built **wfc2gether** (Work From Cafe Together)—a delightful, low-friction virtual study hangout designed to give you the comforting background presence of a bustling neighborhood cafe without ever leaving your desk.

Hop directly into an isometric 3D dollhouse cafe where cute animal companions study alongside you, laptops glow with warm ideas, procedural rain taps gently against the window glass, and synced Pomodoro sprints keep everyone in effortless flow.

## 🎬 Powered by Programmatic Video (Remotion)

Check out our dedicated motion repository: [**`wfc2gether-motion`**](https://github.com/wfc2gether/wfc2gether-motion)

- **100% Programmatic Remotion Architecture:** 35-second high-conversion 9:16 vertical video compositions rendered entirely via React and TypeScript.
- **Organic Vector Physics:** Live-animated vector mascot (*RemotionWuffi*) featuring sinusoidal breathing, responsive winking, waving, coffee-sipping, and MangoBook typing.
- **Zero-Ghosting Series Architecture:** Multi-scene storytelling built with `<Series>` ensuring frame-accurate transitions and zero overlap.

## 🛠️ The Technology Behind the Cozy

- **3D World & Rendering:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) / [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) / [Drei](https://github.com/pmndrs/drei)
- **Frontend Framework:** [Next.js 15](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router) + [React 19](https://react.dev/)
- **Motion & Video Engine:** [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/)
- **State Management:** [Zustand](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand)
- **Design System:** Cozy Kawaii Palette (`#FBF7EE`, `#4E342E`, `#FFE082`, `#81C784`) + [Fredoka](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka) Typography
